The Challenge

Volatile inputs, scattered data, late answers

Procurement buys fuel and additives for many plants from many sources. Finance has to explain cost per tonne against budget. Each team had its own spreadsheets, and the two rarely agreed on the same number at the same time.

Price swings

Petcoke and imported coal prices move with global markets and the rupee; a few dollars a tonne changes margin per bag.

Complex supply network

Fly ash, slag, gypsum, coal and petcoke reach each plant from different sources by rail, road and sea.

Landed cost in pieces

Base price, freight, handling, moisture and calorific value sat in different files, so true landed cost was hard to compare.

Variance explained late

Finance learned why cost per tonne missed budget weeks after month end, too late to act.

Design · One landed-cost definition

Agreed the rules once.

  • Landed cost = price + freight + handling + levies, adjusted for moisture or calorific value.
  • Cost per tonne of cement split by process stage and input.
  • Variance bridge: price, volume, mix, freight, efficiency.
