The Challenge

A full energy portfolio, sold one box at a time

Customers bought a UPS or an inverter. Few saw how solar, storage, backup and charging fit together on their own site.

Nine brochures

Each product had its own leaflet and spec sheet; the system story was left to the salesperson.

Energy flow is invisible

Where solar goes at noon, what the battery does at 7 pm, who pays for the evening peak: hard to explain on paper.

Outages are abstract

Customers did not see that a correctly sized system carries the site through a grid failure.

Sizing came later

Solar and battery sizes and the savings case arrived weeks after the meeting, by spreadsheet.

HMC Approach

From product catalogue to a model customers can walk around

Built with the Power One sales and product teams, and published as one file that opens in any browser.

Discover · What customers ask in the first meeting

Sat in on sales calls across segments.

  • Which products sell together, and which never do.
  • The three questions every customer asks: what does it save, what happens in a power cut, how big.
  • Specifications from the published product pages.

Deliverable: Product-by-site map and pitch storyline.

Design · Four sites, one story

A recognisable building for each segment.

  • Home, shop and office, factory, fuel station.
  • Colour-coded energy flows: solar, battery, grid, EV.
  • Five moves for a meeting: site, tour, outage, day, size.

Deliverable: Storyboard and interaction design.

Build · A browser model, no install

Built with GenAI-assisted coding on WebGL.

  • Procedural 3D buildings, panels, cabinets and chargers.
  • Hourly energy simulation with day, night and outage.
  • Product cards and a sizing calculator inside the model.

Deliverable: Single-file 3D experience.

Adopt · Into every first meeting

Sales opens the site that matches the customer.

  • Guided tour on a laptop or a large screen.
  • Grid-failure moment to make the case for storage.
  • Sizing estimate shared the same day.

Deliverable: Sales playbook.

Scale · Dealers, website and events

The same file travels.

  • Dealer training and showroom screens.
  • Auto-rotating embed for the website and trade shows.
  • New products added as the range grows.

Deliverable: Roll-out kit.
