The Challenge

Thousands of features, a handful of engineers

Inline inspection vendors deliver huge feature lists. Turning them into safe, economical repair decisions was slow, manual and disconnected from what was around the pipe.

Feature lists, not insight

Each run arrived as spreadsheets of depth, length and clock position, reviewed line by line.

Sampled assessments

Fitness-for-service checks were run by hand on the deepest features, so long shallow defects slipped through.

Growth unknown

Repeat runs were not matched, so corrosion rates and remaining life were guesses.

Context missing

River crossings, villages, cathodic protection readings and design factors were not in the same view as the defects.

HMC Approach

Codify the integrity engineer's method

Standard fitness-for-service methods, applied to every feature, in context, with people making the final call.

Discover · How integrity decisions are made

Worked alongside the pipeline integrity team on past runs.

  • Inspection vendor formats, tolerances and feature classes mapped.
  • Past digs compared with predicted severity.
  • Location classes, crossings and cathodic protection survey data collected.

Deliverable: Data model and assessment rulebook.

Design · Standard methods, automated

No black boxes for safety decisions.

  • Modified B31G (0.85 dL) for metal loss, with location-class design factors.
  • Run-to-run matching for corrosion growth rates.
  • Risk = likelihood from remaining life × consequence from location.

Deliverable: Assessment engine specification.

One place from feature to field.

  • 3D defect twin and unrolled wall map.

Key Takeaways

What any pipeline operator can borrow

01

Assess every feature

Automating a standard method removes sampling bias; long, shallow defects get the same scrutiny as deep ones.

02

Growth beats a snapshot

Matched runs turn depth into remaining life, and remaining life into a dig calendar.

03

Context sets the priority

The same defect under a village or a river matters more than in an open field. Put location in the risk score.
