The Challenge

A shoebox of bills, checked by sampling

Sales officers and managers travel across their beats every week. Finance had to settle their claims quickly, fairly and within policy, with only paper and photos to go on.

Bills in every format

Faded thermal slips, handwritten Hindi cash memos and blurry phone photos, all typed in by hand.

Entitlements in a PDF

Caps differ by grade and city tier. Checking each bill against the right cap took time, so most were not.

No way to check where

A dinner bill 200 km from the approved beat looked the same as one next door.

Slow, uneven settlement

Claims waited weeks; deductions arrived without reasons and salespeople pushed back.

HMC Approach

Policy first, then the reader, then the map

We turned the travel policy into rules a computer can apply, taught it to read every bill format the field sends, and added location as a check.

Select a step to see what happened.

Discover · What a claim really looks like

Three months of claims, bills and deductions reviewed with the finance team.

  • Bill formats: GST invoices, cash memos, fuel slips, cab and rail tickets.
  • Where money leaked: over-cap stays, duplicate bills, off-beat meals.
  • Why settlements were slow and disputed.

Deliverable: Leakage map and bill format library.

Design · Policy as rules

Every line of the travel policy written as a check.

  • Caps by grade (M1 to M3) and city tier (A, B, C).
  • Tour plan and geo-fence radius per beat.
  • Clear outcomes: approve, approve to cap, hold, reject, each with a reason.

Deliverable: Rulebook signed off by Finance and Sales.

Build · Reader, checks and map

From photo to decision in seconds.

  • GenAI vision reads printed and handwritten bills, Hindi included.
  • GSTIN check digit, arithmetic, duplicates and dates.
  • Vendor address geocoded and matched to the beat fence and GPS check-ins.

Deliverable: Audit desk and claim register.

Adopt · Finance stays in charge

The desk recommends; Finance decides.

  • Pilot in two zones with weekly settlement.
  • Every deduction comes with a plain-English reason.
  • Holds go back to the salesperson with what is needed.

Deliverable: Pilot results and SOP.

Scale · All sales teams

Same rules for everyone.

  • All zones and grades.
  • Field app sends bills and GPS check-ins together.
  • Approved claims post to the ERP for payment.

Deliverable: Roll-out and monthly audit pack.

03 · Geo-fence

Was the bill issued inside the approved beat?

Each fence is a circle around a town in the day's approved tour plan. Bill pins sit at the vendor's address; small dots are GPS check-ins from the field app. Green pins are inside the fence for that day, red pins are outside.

Bills by distance from fence

Vendor addresses are geocoded (Google Maps Places in production; a built-in gazetteer of UP towns in this demo). "Open in Google Maps" on each pin shows the exact spot.

Key Takeaways

What any finance team can borrow

01

Write the policy as rules first

Once caps, tiers and windows are rules, checking every bill costs the same as checking one.

02

Location is a cheap, strong check

A vendor address plus the day's tour plan answers "was this on the beat?" without accusing anyone.

03

Give a reason with every deduction

Disputes drop when the salesperson sees the rule, the cap and the amount, line by line.
