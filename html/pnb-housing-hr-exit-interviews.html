The Challenge

Exit interviews that told HR nothing

With a large, spread-out workforce of relationship managers, credit and collections staff, HR could not interview every leaver well, and the ones they did rarely got past the polite answer.

Too few interviews

HRBPs covered many branches; most leavers left without a conversation.

Same script for everyone

Fixed forms got fixed answers: "better opportunity", "personal reasons".

Not comfortable with the manager

Leavers held back when the person asking was close to the reason they were leaving.

Insight arrived late

Notes sat in files for weeks; patterns by branch or manager were never seen.

HMC Approach

A good interviewer, available to every leaver

Conversation design first, then voice, then coding the answers into something HR can act on.

Select a step to see what happened.

Design · Five questions, all dynamic

Short, warm and specific.

  • One open question, then four that follow the leaver's answers.
  • Coverage rules: reason, manager, growth, workload, what would have kept them.
  • Guardrails: no pressure to stay, confidential route for misconduct.

Deliverable: Interviewer prompt and guardrails.

Build · Voice, transcript, record

Natural voice and accurate coding.

  • ElevenLabs voice for questions, speech-to-text for answers.
  • GenAI chooses each next question and codes the record.
  • Dashboard by zone, role, tenure and manager.

Deliverable: Working interviewer and dashboard.

Adopt · Pilot with consent

Optional, private and short.

  • Leavers invited on their last week; under 10 minutes.
  • HRBPs review every record and flag.
  • Monthly attrition review uses the dashboard.

Deliverable: Pilot results and HR process.

Scale · From exits to stay interviews

Hear people before they resign.

  • All zones and roles.
  • Manager-level patterns shared with leaders.
  • Same engine for stay interviews with high performers.

Deliverable: Roll-out plan.

Key Takeaways

What any HR team can borrow

01

Follow the answer, not the form

The second question matters most. Asking about what the person just said is what gets past "personal reasons".

02

Neutral, short and optional

Five questions in under ten minutes, without the manager in the room, gets honest answers.

03

Code it, then act on it

A reason only helps when it is counted by branch, role and manager, and someone owns the action.
