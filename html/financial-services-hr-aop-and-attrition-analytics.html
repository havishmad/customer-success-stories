The Challenge

A quarter of the sales force leaving, and no one sure why

Rural Consumer Durable, Rural Liabilities and Rural Term Loan run on large field teams with stretching AOP targets. Exits were rising month on month, recruitment costs were climbing, and retention spend was spread evenly across everyone.

High attrition

Turnover was significant, especially in critical roles. It disrupted operations and pushed up recruitment costs.

Lack of insight

No one understood what was driving exits, so effective retention strategies were hard to design.

Untargeted spend

Money went into generic retention tactics with no clear view of their impact or fit with what employees needed.

Five layers deep

NSM, ZSM, RSM and ASM layers hid where exits clustered. A national average masked teams losing more than half their people.

HMC Approach

From a monthly attrition number to a retention plan per segment

Built with HR business partners and sales leadership, using HRMS records, AOP plans, performance data, engagement surveys and exit interviews.

Select a step to see what happened.

Discover · Join the data

Employee records, AOP plans and exit data lived in separate systems.

  • Joined HRMS headcount, monthly exits, band, age and tenure.
  • Mapped every sales officer to their ASM, RSM, ZSM and NSM.
  • Coded exit-interview reasons and linked survey scores to individuals.

Deliverable: One employee-month table, from April onwards.

Design · Agree the measures

Leaders agreed one definition before arguing about the number.

  • Annualized attrition = exits ÷ average headcount × 12 ÷ months elapsed.
  • AOP achievement measured at the ASM territory level.
  • Early attrition = exits within six months of joining.

Deliverable: Metric dictionary and dashboard wireframes.

Build · Dashboard and model

A Power BI dashboard for leaders and a risk model for HR.

  • Pages: Home, Attrition, Chain, Decomposition tree, Scatter AOP + Attrition.
  • Predictive model scoring every employee's likelihood of leaving.
  • Segments with a matched retention action and cost per head.

Deliverable: Power BI report, risk scores and segment playbook.

Adopt · Monthly people review

Attrition joined the sales review, not just the HR review.

  • NSMs and ZSMs review their own decomposition tree every month.
  • HRBPs act on the high-risk list before resignations arrive.
  • Retention budget allocated by segment, not spread evenly.

Deliverable: Review cadence and HRBP action tracker.

Scale · Beyond rural sales

The same model extends to other businesses and roles.

  • Monthly model refresh with new joiners and exits.
  • Effect of each retention action tracked against a control group.
  • AOP setting informed by attrition risk in each territory.

Deliverable: Refresh routine and measurement plan.

Key Takeaways

What any sales-led business can borrow

01

Attrition is local

A small share of teams drove a large share of exits. The decomposition tree turned a national problem into named teams and named actions.

02

Targets and exits move together

Teams far below AOP lost people fastest, so target setting became a retention lever rather than just a sales one.

03

Spend where the risk is

Risk scores let HR fund the right action for the right segment, and the simulator showed the return before any money moved.
