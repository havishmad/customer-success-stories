The Challenge

Defects fixed, then back next month

Every insulation line produces thousands of kilometres of wire a month. A single pinhole means cutting out a length; thin insulation can scrap a whole coil.

Data in silos

Rejection registers, spark-tester counts and extruder settings lived in different sheets and logbooks.

Fixing the loudest, not the costliest

Teams chased the defect seen most recently, not the one costing the most metres and rupees.

Fishbones from memory

Cause lists were brainstormed in meetings; few causes were ever tested against data.

8D reports took weeks

Customer complaints waited around three weeks for a root cause and corrective action report.

HMC Approach

Join the data, then let it rank the causes

    What any plant can borrow

    01

    Rank by rupees, not by noise

    The Pareto in money terms tells the team which single defect to fix first.

    02

    Make every cause testable

    A fishbone cause that can't be checked against data is an opinion. Write each one as a test.

    A root cause is confirmed only when the chart shows the process staying in control afterwards.
