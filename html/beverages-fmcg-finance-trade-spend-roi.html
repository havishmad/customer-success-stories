The Challenge

Big spend, thin evidence

Finance could say what was paid. It could not say what the payment bought.

Volume, not uplift

Schemes were judged on total cases sold, including cases that would have sold anyway.

Paying for the baseline

Big outlets crossed slabs on natural growth and collected payouts for doing nothing different.

Claims on trust

Thousands of distributor claims a month; only a small sample checked by hand.

Accrual by guesswork

Month-end provisions were trued up a quarter later, swinging margins.
