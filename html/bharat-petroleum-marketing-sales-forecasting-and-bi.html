The Challenge

Lots of sales data, no single view of it

Every sales office sent its own file. Product names, sales groups and offices were kept in separate lists. Before anyone could ask "how are we tracking against last year?", someone had to spend days building the table to answer it.

Scattered data

Product, sales-code and numerous sales files sat in a folder, each in its own shape.

No central analytics

Without one model, insight was hard to get and decisions leaned on whichever spreadsheet was closest.

April, not January

BPCL's year runs April to March. Calendar-year dates in reports put quarters and weeks in the wrong buckets.

Manual, every month

Copying, cleaning and recalculating consumed time and resources that should have gone into growth.

HMC Approach

Model once, refresh forever

Built with BPCL's sales analytics team, on Power BI and the files they already received.

Select a step to see what happened.

Discover · Map the files

We listed every file, master and spreadsheet that fed sales reporting.

  • Monthly sales files by office, with product codes and sales-group codes.
  • Separate product and sales-group masters.
  • Several local definitions of quarter and week.

Deliverable: Source inventory and data dictionary.

Design · A star schema

One fact table, three dimensions, one direction of filtering.

  • Fact: date, company, product, sales district, office, SG, state, sales.
  • Dim_Product, Dim_SGCode and a Calendar table.
  • Fiscal year starting in April; weeks starting on Monday.

Deliverable: Data model and measure list.

Build · Power Query, DAX, forecast

Three layers, each with one job.

  • Power Query appends every file in the folder and joins the masters.
  • A DAX calendar with fiscal month, quarter, week and year labels.
  • Measures for YoY, FYTD and trend, plus a forecast with confidence bands.

Deliverable: Power BI model and Forecast dashboard.

Adopt · Refresh, then read

New files go into the folder; the dashboard refreshes.

  • Sales leaders filter by date range and case origin.
  • Forecasts reviewed in the monthly business review.
  • Calendar parameters documented for future changes.

Deliverable: Refresh routine and user walkthrough.

Scale · Same model, more questions

The model became the base for more reports.

  • New dimensions plug into the same star.
  • Calendar reused across other Power BI reports.
  • Forecast extended to product and region level.

Deliverable: Reusable model template.
