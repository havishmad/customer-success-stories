The Challenge

Lighting is won or lost long before the tender

In projects business, the brand a consultant specifies, the contractor who buys and the discount offered decide the outcome. BMTC's leaders wanted to see the whole pipeline and bid with evidence, not instinct.

Pipeline in many heads

Project leads sat with individual salespeople and spreadsheets, with no single view across emirates.

Specification blind spots

Which consultants specify BMTC brands, and where competitors are written in, was not tracked.

Discounting by habit

Deep discounts were given to win, even where specification meant the order was likely anyway.

Slow tender preparation

Reading tender packs and finding comparable past bids took days for each enquiry.
