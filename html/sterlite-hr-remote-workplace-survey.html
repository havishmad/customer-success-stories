The Challenge

A workplace decision that had to be made role by role

Leadership needed to decide who works from home, from a Sterlite office or from a satellite office near home. Preferences alone would not do; the answer depended on what each role actually needs.

No one-size answer

Some roles need office infrastructure or in-person meetings; others can run fully remote.

Clean input at scale

Every manager had to answer the same questions, rank options properly and assess each team member.

Many separate files

Each manager returned their own protected workbook, saved under their own name.

Comparable scores

Answers had to become one score per role that could be sliced by BU, function, grade and life stage.

Design · Two sections, one index

Manager views plus a role-by-role assessment.

  • Section 01: manager views, ranking of office uses and satellite locations.
  • Section 02: each team member's role scored on five factors.
  • Weights turn the five factors into a 1–5 remote-readiness index.

Deliverable: Questionnaire and scoring rules.

Build · Form, macro, model

Every step automated.

  • Excel VBA survey with login, validation and guided save.
  • Consolidation macro that loops through every returned file.
  • Power Query shaping and a Power BI model with employee and manager databases.

Deliverable: Survey workbook, macro, Power BI report.

Adopt · Run the survey

Managers filled the form and mailed it back.

  • Personalised passwords sent by email.
  • Validation stopped incomplete or duplicate rankings.
  • HR dropped returned files into one folder and ran the macro.

Deliverable: Consolidated responses.

Scale · Decide and revisit

The dashboard drove the workplace plan.

  • Satellite office locations ranked by managers.
  • Same pipeline reusable for future pulse surveys.

      Key Takeaways

      What any HR team can borrow

      01

      Assess roles, not just preferences

      Asking managers what each role needs gave a defensible basis for who works where.

      02

      Validate at the point of entry

      Unique ranks and complete answers at submit time removed most of the cleaning later.

      03

      Make the score transparent

      Five named factors and visible weights let leadership see, and challenge, how the index works.
