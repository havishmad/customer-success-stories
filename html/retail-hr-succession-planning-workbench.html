The Challenge

Succession lived in eight spreadsheets and a lot of free text

Each concept kept its own talent file, with 35 columns from performance history to CEO comments. Pulling it together for the annual talent review took weeks, and the most useful information was in long notes nobody had time to read.

Fragmented data

Every concept had its own version of the template, with different spellings, bands and rating scales.

Notes nobody read

Strengths, development areas and successor notes ran to paragraphs. Calibration meetings ran out of time before they reached them.

Hidden risk

Critical roles without a successor, held by someone at high flight risk, were not visible until someone resigned.

Slow review cycle

Consolidating, cleaning and building slides for the CEO review took about nine weeks every year.

HMC Approach

Clean once, view many ways, summarise with AI

We kept HR's template and rating scales, cleaned the data once, and built the views the CEO, concept heads and HR business partners actually ask for.

Select a step to see what happened.

Discover · Read every column

We mapped the 35-column template with group HR and concept HR heads.

  • Agreed definitions: critical role, HIPO, consistent performer, weak link.
  • Listed the questions the CEO asks in a talent review, and which columns answer them.
  • Found the cleaning rules: band, function and concept names.

Deliverable: KPI dictionary and data-cleaning rules.

Design · Six views, one dataset

Each view answers one kind of question.

  • Concept view and KPI dashboard for "where is our risk?".
  • Employee list and 9-box for "who are our people?".
  • Succession pipeline and full data view for "who is next?" and "show me the detail".

Deliverable: Wireframes signed off by Group HR.

Build · A single offline file

Built with Claude as one HTML file: no server, no install.

  • Filters by concept, role level and year, carried across views.
  • Click any KPI to see the people behind the number.
  • Optional GenAI summaries using HR's own API key and prompt.

Deliverable: Succession workbench and refresh guide.

Adopt · Run the talent review from it

Calibration and the CEO review ran live from the workbench.

  • HRBPs set status, priority and remarks during calibration.
  • AI summaries replaced reading paragraphs aloud.
  • Actions agreed in the room, exported straight after.

Deliverable: Calibrated talent decisions for all concepts.

Scale · From annual to always-on

The same file is refreshed each quarter.

  • Quarterly refresh of ratings, attrition risk and readiness.
  • Year-on-year 9-box movement tracked automatically.
  • Next: link development plans and retention actions to outcomes.

Deliverable: Quarterly talent pulse.

The Solution

The workbench. Click around.

Six tabs on one dataset of 35 columns. Filter by concept, click any KPI to see the people behind it, switch the 9-box year to watch people move, open anyone's profile, and use ⚙ AI settings to add your own OpenAI key and prompt for summaries.

Key Takeaways

What transfers to any HR team

01

Define the KPIs before the dashboard

Agreeing what "critical", "HIPO" and "weak link" mean did more for the review than any chart.

02

Every number should open to names

Leaders trust a KPI when one click shows the people behind it.

03

Let AI read the notes

The richest talent data is free text. Summaries in HR's own words put it back into the conversation.
