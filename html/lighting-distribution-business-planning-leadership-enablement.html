The Challenge

Planning a distribution business in a fast-moving market

A lighting and electrical distributor plans across many categories, brands and project pipelines. Leaders wanted to use GenAI, but needed it grounded in their own planning work, not generic demos.

Planning by spreadsheet

Category plans built bottom-up in workbooks, with scenarios slow to run and harder to explain.

GenAI curiosity, little practice

Leaders had tried chat tools, but not on planning, pricing or customer proposals.

Value story hard to tell

Energy savings sell lighting, but quantifying them for each customer took specialist time.

Slow quotations

Matching consultant BOQs to the catalogue line by line delayed responses to tenders.

HMC Approach

Learn on your own problems, leave with something working

Every session used the distributor's planning questions and data patterns, so leaders practised on the work they do.

Select a step to see what happened.

Discover · Baseline and one-to-ones

Started with the people.

  • GenAI baseline assessment and pre-workshop pulse check.
  • One-to-one calls with leaders on their planning pain points.
  • Use-case assessment to pick problems worth solving.

Deliverable: Readiness baseline and use-case long list.

Design · A programme around planning

Built the curriculum backwards from the annual plan.

  • Sessions mapped to planning tasks: market scan, category plans, scenarios, proposals.
  • Problems built on distributor-style data.
  • HMC safety framework for responsible use.

Deliverable: Programme design and session packs.

Build · Hands-on workshops

Leaders did the work in the room.

  • Prompting for planning: structure, context, constraints, format.
  • Scenario planning and plan narratives with GenAI.
  • Prototyping customer tools with GenAI-assisted building.

Deliverable: Prompts, workflows and prototypes.

Adopt · Hackathon and showcase

Teams presented working prototypes.

  • Cross-functional teams built on real problems.
  • Leadership panel judged value and feasibility.
  • Assets captured in a reusable repository.

Deliverable: Prototype showcase and asset library.
