The Challenge

By the time an account was overdue, the best moment had passed

Risk was measured in days past due, fraud checks looked at one file at a time, and collections treated every customer in a bucket the same way.

Late detection

Stress showed up as a missed EMI. Salary delays, bounces and new borrowing that came before it were not tracked together.

Fraud hidden in the links

Each application looked clean on its own. The shared phones, devices, valuers and employers only showed across files.

One-size collections

Agents worked lists by bucket and amount. Customers who would self-cure got calls; customers who needed a plan got pressure.

Conduct risk

Recovery messages and calls must follow fair-practice rules on timing, tone and privacy. Checks were manual and sample-based.

Key Takeaways

What any lender can borrow

01

Signals beat buckets

The month before a missed EMI usually tells the story. Score it, explain it and act before the bucket changes.

02

Fraud hides in the links

A clean file can sit inside a dirty network. Check what applications share, and know which links are harmless.

03

Right treatment, within the rules

Match the channel to the customer, help those in hardship, and check every message for fair practice before it goes out.
